# PixelByte art and game feel

Version 0.3 uses a warm, quiet palette: sage and teal surfaces, cream typography, lavender accents, oak, and morning sunlight. The environment carries the detail; menus use restrained spacing, clear hierarchy, and a consistent gold primary action. Secondary information stays at the edges of the full-screen office. The quest tracker yields when the player walks underneath it.

The workstation editor is styled as a small PixelByte desktop computer: oak-colored bezel, lined-paper ticket, sage accents, a stippled title ribbon, and a deep-green CRT with phosphor scan lines. Its pixel-grid backdrop, clipped block borders, and hard-edged panel shadows carry the office palette through the readable code area. Furniture surfaces stay in the office illustration behind the actor; only narrow front edges are drawn in the foreground. Near a desk or table, the player faces the room so both eyes remain visible, even while sidestepping. The office avatar is hidden behind the focused editor overlay, so it cannot overlap the monitor or chair; the saved walking position stays unchanged. Movement uses normalized input, exponential acceleration/braking, distance-based strides, and a bounded camera with velocity lead. Interaction selection has hysteresis to avoid flicker at the edge of a target. Dialogue reserves the height of its complete text, reveals one character every 19 ms, and lets E, Enter, Space, or the button reveal/advance. Menus use a shared display clock with a native WebKit watchdog. Essential feedback never depends on an opacity animation completing. System Reduce Motion removes decorative movement and makes dialogue/menu reveals immediate.

## Asset provenance

- Production environment: `public/art/pixelbyte-office-v3.png`, 1586 × 992, generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool using the imagegen skill. This is an original generated environment, not a third-party game asset.
- Input reference: a project-authored SVG floor-plan/blockout, rendered to PNG before generation. It established the room layout and furniture locations. No reference-game screenshots were supplied.
- Original generated output: `01a17947-4881-7312-b924-d13e267fe9bf/exec-cc165128-2be6-4b45-8112-d861f71d37ed.png` in the local generated-images library; the selected image is copied into the project for offline packaging.
- Characters, avatar customization, walking/typing/talking/coffee gestures, lighting effects, dust, steam, reward props, interface icons, and the fallback office are drawn in code. Generated pixels do not implement collision or interaction: the logical map, furniture foreground crops, and actor positions are maintained explicitly in `src/game`.
- Sound cues are synthesized locally with Web Audio and respect the saved sound preference.

The output is a production bitmap, not an exact geometric rendering of the input blockout. Collision footprints and foreground layers were adjusted to its actual furniture placement. The first-day reward props remain dynamic gameplay elements.
